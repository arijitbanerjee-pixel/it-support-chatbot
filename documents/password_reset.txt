Razorpay IT Password Reset Procedure

If you have forgotten your company password, follow these steps:

1. Open the company password portal.
2. Select "Forgot Password".
3. Enter your company username.
4. Complete the identity verification process.
5. Enter a new password.
6. Confirm the new password.
7. Sign in again using the new password.

Password requirements:
- Minimum 12 characters.
- At least one uppercase letter.
- At least one lowercase letter.
- At least one number.
- At least one special character.

If password reset fails after three attempts, contact the IT Support team.
